Your AI agent is writing code right now.
But you have no idea what it's doing.






A blinking cursor. Scrolling text. Maybe some logs.
Zero visual feedback.


Then I found a VS Code extension called Pixel Agents.
It turns your AI coding agents into animated pixel art characters
sitting in a virtual office.

Your agent writes code, the character types at a desk.
Your agent searches files, the character reads documents.
Your agent goes idle, the character walks to the break room.

It does nothing productive.
But developers can't stop watching it.

As AI agents become teammates and not just tools,
we need better ways to see what they're doing.

I broke it all down in the PDF attached to this post.
Give it a read.
