My calendar was full. My progress was empty.


9-to-5 job. Freelance clients. Content plan. Interview prep. A fitness routine I kept rescheduling to "next Monday."

Every Sunday I'd plan the week. Color-coded blocks. Time slots for everything. Looked beautiful. My Google Calendar belonged in a museum.

By Wednesday it was fiction.

Here's what was happening: I was scheduling tasks, not priorities.

"Reply to client emails" got a time slot.
"Actually build the thing the client is paying for" got pushed to "later."

"Watch tutorial on system design" made the calendar.
"Apply to jobs that pay double my salary" kept getting moved to next week.

Busy every day. Going nowhere.

Then I read this Covey line and it stung: "The key is not to prioritize what's on your schedule, but to schedule your priorities."

I flipped it. Started each week with one question: "What are the 3 things that actually matter this week?"

Everything else gets whatever time is left. Or doesn't get done.

Nobody died because I replied to that email 6 hours late. Nobody even noticed.

. Busy and productive are not the same thing. I proved this for six months.
. If your top priority doesn't have a time block, it's not really a priority.
. Your calendar shows what you actually value. Look at it honestly.

"The key is not to prioritize what's on your schedule, but to schedule your priorities." - Stephen Covey

Sticking this on my monitor. Again.
