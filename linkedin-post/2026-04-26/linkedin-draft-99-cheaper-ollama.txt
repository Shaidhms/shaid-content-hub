I use Claude Max and I'm not switching. That's my honest starting point.

But when I saw this setup — Ollama running open source models locally, almost zero cost — I had to actually test it instead of dismissing it.

What I found: it's not a replacement. But it's not a toy either.

For certain types of work, open source models hold their own. For others, Opus is worth every rupee and the comparison isn't close.

The more useful thing I figured out was where the line actually is. Because "use open source to save money" without knowing the tradeoffs is how you slow yourself down on work that matters.

Two setups that work:
- Ollama locally, if your hardware can handle it
- Open Router for free cloud models when it can't

Where they fall short compared to Opus is just as important as where they work.
