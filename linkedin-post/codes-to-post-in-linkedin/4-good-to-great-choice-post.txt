Nobody handed me a Gen AI career. I chose it.



A year ago I was a performance testing consultant.
15 years in the same domain. Good at it. Comfortable.
The kind of comfortable that slowly turns into invisible.

I could have stayed. The money was fine. The work was familiar.
But I kept looking at the AI space and thinking "I should be in there."

So I made a choice. Started learning. Started building. Started posting.
With a full-time job. With bills. With zero AI portfolio.

"Greatness is not a function of circumstance. Greatness is largely a matter of conscious choice." - Jim Collins, Good to Great

I'm not calling myself great. Far from it.
But I did make a choice that scared me. And that choice changed everything.

The people I admire in tech didn't get lucky.
They made a decision when it would have been easier not to.

. They chose to build when consuming was more comfortable
. They chose to post when staying silent was safer
. They chose to start over when staying put paid better

Your circumstances are real. I'm not pretending they aren't.
But within those constraints, you still have choices.

What's the one choice you've been putting off?
