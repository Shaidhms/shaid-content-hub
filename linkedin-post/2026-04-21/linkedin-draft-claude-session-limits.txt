98.5% of your Claude tokens are doing nothing useful.






They're just rereading what you already said.


One developer tracked a 100+ message chat and found that only 1.5% of the tokens produced new output.
The rest?
Rereading history. Over and over and every single turn.

That stat stopped me mid-session.

I used to think hitting session limits was a plan problem.
Upgrade the plan. Bigger window. Problem solved.
Then I read Anthropic's own guidance and realised it's a hygiene problem.

Claude rereads the entire conversation every message.
So your cost doesn't add up.
It compounds.

Message 1 might cost 500 tokens.
Message 30 costs 15,500.
After a 30-message session you're already sitting on 232K cumulative tokens.

And here's the part no one talks about.

Retrieval accuracy drops from 92% at 256K tokens to 78% at 1M tokens.
Bigger window does not mean better output.
It just gives the model more room to get distracted.


The 1M token window is insurance, not a goal.
The first 20% of any session is prime time.
That's where CLAUDE.md is freshest and the model is most primed.

Treat context like a workspace, not a warehouse.
