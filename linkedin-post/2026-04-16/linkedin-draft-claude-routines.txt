Anthropic shipped Routines. My laptop is finally off the hook.






For months I had a little graveyard of Claude Code scripts sitting on my Mac.
A deploy checker. A standup drafter. A dependency watcher.
All of them paused the second I closed the lid.

Routines move that work to Anthropic's infrastructure.
Save a prompt, attach a repo, pick a trigger. Done.
It runs whether I'm asleep, boarding a flight, or nowhere near my desk.

Cron was always too dumb for what I actually wanted.
Full agents were too loose.
Routines sit between those two, and that middle is the part I care about.

Migrating one boring task this week. We'll see what breaks.
