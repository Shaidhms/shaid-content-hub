Pro Tip: Speed Boost
  • After your first chat, go to Settings
  • Find 'KV Cache Quantization'
  • Switch from f16 to q4_0
  • This roughly triples inference speed with minimal quality loss
  • First message is always slower (model loads into memory)
  • Subsequent messages are much faster
